# car.glb

"Car Concept" from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It started from a public-domain (CC0) model by Unity Fan on Sketchfab.

Changes: compressed with gltf-transform (meshopt geometry, WebP textures at 1024 px). In the game, the licence
plate and steering-wheel emblem are hidden, because the licence excludes the Khronos logos on them, and the
transmission glass is replaced with tinted glass.
